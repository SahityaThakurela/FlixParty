Privacy Policy for FlixParty

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

FlixParty ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a browser extension designed to allow friends to watch Netflix and YouTube videos together in synchronized real-time playback with peer-to-peer audio, video, and text chat.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

FlixParty is built with a privacy-first, peer-to-peer architecture:

  • We do not store, record, or track your browsing history or personal data.
  • We do not record or store your webcam video or microphone audio. Video and voice streams are transmitted directly between party participants via encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer connections.
  • We do not collect or sell any personal information to third parties.

2. Local Storage Usage

FlixParty stores only user configuration preferences locally on your browser using chrome.storage.local:

  • Your chosen display nickname.
  • Your chosen signaling server URL.
  • Your preferred default playback control mode (Everyone vs Host-Only).

This data never leaves your device except to identify your nickname to your party participants.

3. Permissions Used

  • storage: To save your local preferences (nickname, server URL).
  • tabs & activeTab: To detect current playback URL on supported streaming services (Netflix, YouTube) to keep playback synchronized.
  • camera & microphone: Requested optionally for in-party video call and voice chat.
